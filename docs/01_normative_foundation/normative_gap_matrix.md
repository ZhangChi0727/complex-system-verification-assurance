# Normative Gap Matrix

本矩阵只记录会影响 Framework 架构或后续研究顺序的实质缺口。缺口表示截至当前已研究规范范围内，某项 Framework concern 尚未得到充分定义、支持或约束，并不表示相关标准本身不完整。

| ID | Framework Topic | Normative Source | Normative Statement/Object | Interpretation | Current DCAS Practice | Gap | Proposed Framework Response | Confidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ISO-G01 | Verification independence | ISO 15288:2023, 6.3.8; 6.4.9; ARP4754B, 5.2, 5.7, Appendix A; ARP4761A, 2.2, Appendices E, J–M, P | ISO only establishes QA independence; aviation sources distinguish objective/process independence and functional, item-development, physical and process forms with contextual substantiation. | Typed aviation independence cannot be relabeled as a universal verification rule or flattened into one scalar. | `independence_requirement` is a scalar candidate field. | Generic independence remains unresolved and the current representation is too coarse. | Keep a generic constraint extension point; model typed aviation independence objects/claims/evidence; await item standards before freezing. | High | Partially Supported |
| ISO-G02 | Verification coverage | ISO 15288:2023, 5.10; 6.4.9; ARP4754B, 5.5.5.2.2; ARP4761A, D.5, E.4, F.4 | ISO has no universal taxonomy; aviation sources cover requirements and assess FC/objective/requirement/assumption/independence populations. | These are aviation dimensions, not a universal taxonomy, percentage or sufficiency proof. | `coverage_obligations` is a candidate list without a frozen taxonomy. | Cross-domain coverage types and completeness rules remain unresolved. | Preserve pluggable obligations; add an aviation safety-coverage profile and await item-level standards. | High | Partially Supported |

| ISO-G07 | Information-item schema | ISO 15288:2023, 5.6; 6.3.6; Annex B; ISO 24748-1:2024, 6.2.8; ISO 24748-2:2024, 6.7.4, 6.8; ARP4754B, 5.4.7, 5.5.6, Appendix A | Sources support controlled planning/data relationships; ARP names aviation outputs and Appendix A maps content/objectives, but no source mandates the repository's unified schema. | Record need or example content does not make every repository field normative. | Repository templates define specific fields and record names. | Cross-standard field semantics and cardinalities remain incomplete. | Keep field provenance; study ISO 15289 and later aviation standards before schema freeze. | High | Partially Supported |
| ISO-G08 | MBSE automation and model evidence | ISO 15288:2023, Annex D; ISO 24748-1:2024, Annex A.10; ARP4761A, Appendix N | Models can support checking/analysis; ARP4761A adds technology-neutral MBSA model verification, baselines, reproducibility and limitations, while explicitly not requiring MBSE. | MBSA evidence controls do not establish a generic language, tool qualification or evidence-admissibility rule. | MBSE metamodel and automation are planned. | Cross-domain model validity and evidence admissibility rules remain unresolved. | Reuse MBSA lessons in a future model-evidence profile; preserve MBSA ≠ MBSE and require model-validation evidence before automated claims. | High | Partially Supported |
| LC-G01 | Gate semantics | ISO 24748-1:2024, 4.3; Clause 5; 6.2.6 | Entry/exit criteria, reviews/milestones and decision gates are related lifecycle-management concepts. | Criteria satisfaction, review completion and authorization decision are not one event. | V6 and V12 are named as if each were one activity. | Composite state/event semantics and decision authority are unresolved. | Model V6/V12 as composites with separately traceable assessment, optional review, decision and baseline events. | High | Framework Implication |
| LC-G02 | Review taxonomy | ISO 24748-1:2024, 6.4; Annex C; Annex F, F.3.6 | Annex F provides candidate joint stakeholder reviews; F.3.6 defines `Verification reviews`, not a mandatory `Verification Readiness Review`. | Lifecycle review is not the same abstraction as Verification Method inspection/peer review. | `Verification Readiness` may be read as a mandatory VRR. | Review levels, naming and authority remain domain/project dependent. | Maintain separate lifecycle-review and verification-method taxonomies; classify VRR as proposal until later sources support it. | High | Framework Implication |
| LC-G03 | Process-view provenance | ISO 24748-1:2024, Annex D | A process view organizes selected activities/tasks from source processes but does not define new ones. | Framework orchestration objects require explicit source/proposal classification. | V0–V12 currently mix activities, evaluations, gates and orchestration. | Source-task provenance and ontology are incomplete. | Require every V-element to identify ontology, source mapping and framework-added behavior. | High | Framework Implication |
| LC-G04 | Lifecycle/process instantiation evidence | ISO 24748-1:2024, 6.2.2–6.2.8; ISO 24748-2:2024, 6.4, 6.7.4.1, 6.8 | Lifecycle/process selection, strategy integration, mappings and adaptation rationale should be reflected in project planning. | 24748-2 validates the record concept but still does not mandate a universal schema. | A research-draft tailoring/instantiation record now exists. | Approval/cardinality and profile-extension rules are not frozen. | Retain the candidate record and validate it through project use and ISO 15289 research. | High | Partially Supported |
| ARP-G01 | Assurance applicability and rigor | ARP4754B, 5.2; 5.6.4; Appendix A; ARP4761A, 3.9; Appendix P | ARP4754B relates FDAL to objective applicability/independence/control; ARP4761A assigns/reassesses FDAL/IDAL using FC, architecture and independence. | These remain distinct, provenance-bearing aviation attributes; certification-credit intent is a separate project-use relation. | VSR previously risked flattening applicability and certification-use semantics. | Item-level objective links and final record semantics remain unresolved. | Keep typed aviation Assurance Constraints and separate certification-credit intent; freeze after DO-178C/DO-254 review. | High | Partially Supported |
| ARP-G02 | Cross-level verification credit | ARP4754B, 4.6.1; 5.5.4; 5.5.6 | Aircraft/system activities may be allocated to item level and item data accepted upward when planned and substantiated. | Allocation, execution, evidence ownership and acceptance are separate relationships. | Allocation/execution levels exist but delegation/acceptance/credit do not. | Cross-level conclusions can lose provenance or be double-counted. | Model `allocated_to`, `performed_at`, `evidence_from`, `accepted_by` and `credit_basis` as candidate relations. | High | Research Proposal |
| ARP-G03 | Unintended-behavior assurance | ARP4754B, 4.6.4; 5.5.5.3; Appendix A Objective 5.2; ARP4761A, Section 4 | Verification can identify/address unintended behavior; safety-analysis methods can characterize failure propagation and common causes. | This is an aviation assurance concern, not a universal proof of absence; safety analysis does not replace item verification. | An optional aviation obligation exists without frozen coverage rules. | Item-level applicability, method diversity and sufficiency remain unresolved. | Retain optional aviation-profile obligation and test it against DO-178C/DO-254. | Medium | Partially Supported |
| SAF-G01 | Safety-to-verification obligation derivation | ARP4761A, 2.2; 3.2–3.5; D.4.3 | FC/classification establishes objective basis; a Safety Requirement may achieve an objective or satisfy a Safety Process constraint. PASA/PSSA derive and allocate requirements from objectives, independence principles, controlled assumptions and architecture/analysis results. | A Failure Condition is upstream; multi-source provenance must converge through a traceable Requirement or Constraint before an obligation is formed. | No typed multi-source safety provenance model exists. | Linear provenance can discard constraint-, independence-, assumption- or architecture-derived origins and weaken change impact. | Add typed `SafetyRequirementOrigin` relations, then `SafetyRequirement or AssuranceConstraint → Verification/AssuranceObligation`; prohibit direct FC→obligation generation. | High | Research Proposal |
| SAF-G02 | Assumption lifecycle | ARP4761A, A.6; D.4.3.2; D.5; E.4 | Assumptions are captured, propagated, converted where controllable, confirmed/corrected and reassessed. | Generic promotion is not yet justified by one domain source. | VSR has an untyped `assumptions` list. | Ownership, confirmation obligations, validity and impact are not represented. | Add aviation candidate `Assumption`, `Assumption Obligation`, `Assumption Confirmation`; review generic promotion later. | High | Research Proposal |
| SAF-G03 | Multi-type independence | ARP4761A, 2.2; Appendices E, J–M, P | Functional, item-development, physical and process independence are distinct; principles, requirements, claims and evidence differ. | The types and substantiation methods are aviation-profile semantics. | One scalar field conflates types and states. | Independence rationale and evidence cannot be audited reliably. | Replace the scalar in the aviation extension with typed constraints/claims and substantiation references. | High | Research Proposal |
| SAF-G04 | Safety evidence aggregation | ARP4761A, E.3–E.5; F.3–F.5 | SSA/ASA combine development verification results and safety-analysis results into traceable safety assessment outputs. | Aggregation does not make every result identical to Evidence. | Evidence taxonomy lacks safety source/aggregation semantics. | Provenance, configuration, assumptions and cross-level acceptance may be lost. | Add candidate Development Verification, Safety Analysis and Safety Assessment Evidence roles. | High | Research Proposal |
| SAF-G05 | Safety sufficiency reasoning | ARP4761A, D.5; E.4; F.4 | Completion evaluates heterogeneous qualitative/quantitative, DAL, independence, assumption, problem and traceability results. | No single metric or probability proves sufficiency; criteria are aviation-specific. | V11 has no safety profile. | The framework cannot explain why combined safety evidence is adequate. | Add aviation V11 criteria and an explicit reasoning/limitations record; retain generic sufficiency gap. | High | Research Proposal |
| SAF-G06 | Safety-assessment/change synchronization | ARP4761A, 3.1.1; A.6; E.4; P.1 | Design/assessment/assumption changes propagate and can require PSSA/SSA and DAL reassessment. | Trigger/selection rules remain programme-dependent. | V10 lacks an explicit safety subflow. | Development re-verification and safety reassessment can diverge. | Add typed V10 safety-impact, reassessment, evidence-validity and synchronization relations. | High | Research Proposal |
