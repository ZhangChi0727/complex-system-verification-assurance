## Research orientation

执行本任务前必须先读取本节与 frontmatter dependencies，并带着明确的研究问题、候选反证路径和来源边界进入原文。任务不是一般性总结，也不是为当前框架寻找支持。

### Task purpose and research attitude

以 2025 版建立当前 assurance vocabulary 和 Part-family concept basis，并只对仓库实际使用的 Claim、assurance、uncertainty 概念执行限定兼容性检查。

本任务服务于 `research_scope.md` 的三层目标：产品无关 Verification Methodology -> Model-Based Verification Architecture -> 非产品化 Verification Platform 研究原型。标准明确规定的内容形成构建约束；标准没有回答的内容形成 gap 或 innovation-space observation。`Standard wins over current framework hypothesis`；标准沉默不得由 agent 补齐，也不得作为 novelty proof。

### Source-specific research entry

原文目录包括 Clause 3 assurance/system-life-cycle/integrity/risk terms、Clause 4 basic concepts、Clause 5 multi-part relationships/authorities、Clause 6 assurance case/reasoning/evidence/certification、Clause 7 integrity levels/risk 与 Clause 8 lifecycle assurance。

该结构说明研究入口，不构成条款结论。Agent 必须在 source gate 后建立完整 inventory，并允许实际条款内容修正本说明中的初始假设。

### Research questions carried by this task

- **RQ4**: 如何定义 Verification Sufficiency，且为什么 Requirement Coverage 本身不足以证明充分性？ 本任务只回答与本来源及其受控 scope 直接相关的子问题；完整答案由 Task 022 综合。
- **RQ5**: Verification Evidence 如何通过可审查的 Assurance Argument 支持 Compliance Claim？ 本任务只回答与本来源及其受控 scope 直接相关的子问题；完整答案由 Task 022 综合。

### Innovation candidates under test

- **INN-T1** — Verification Sufficiency 的显式推理接口与残差处理语义。Falsification condition: 既有通用标准或文献已给出等价、可执行且跨证据类型的完整语义。Non-claim: 不宣称已研究标准未定义 sufficiency 即证明原创。执行时必须比较 purpose、objects、relations、lifecycle、authority 和 applicability，而不是只比较标签。
- **INN-T3** — Evidence identity、provenance、applicability、credibility 与 sufficiency 的分离模型。Falsification condition: 既有 assurance/evidence metamodel 已提供等价谓词和关系。Non-claim: 不把 Evidence 或 Claim-Argument-Evidence 概念本身主张为原创。执行时必须比较 purpose、objects、relations、lifecycle、authority 和 applicability，而不是只比较标签。

Tasks 001-021 只能返回 `SUPPORT / QUALIFY / FALSIFY / NO EVIDENCE`；Task 022 只能综合为 `SUPPORTED / QUALIFIED / FALSIFIED / OPEN`。任何任务都不能设置 `novelty established`。

### Evidence hierarchy

1. target-source normative provisions;
2. target-source informative material;
3. independently reviewed repository research on other normative sources;
4. directly checked practice-comparison sources;
5. framework hypotheses and research proposals.

低层级证据可以提出问题、比较维度或反例，但不得覆盖或替代高层级证据。Informative material 不得转成 requirement；reviewed note 不替代本任务原文研究；practice source 不生成 target-source clause record；framework hypothesis 不决定标准应当说什么。来源数量不能替代证据质量，禁止来源投票。

### Durable research interface

本任务产生的 reviewed note 是下游研究的耐久接口。执行者必须研究完整受控原文，独立评审者必须使用自己合法取得且指纹匹配的原文核验关键 locator；评审通过后，下游任务应只消费 note 中的 locator + faithful paraphrase + source class + object/relation + limitation + review disposition，而不重新进行同一标准的全文研究。研究笔记不得以“见原文”代替命题，也不取代正式标准。

### Core research principles

1. `Standard evidence -> interpretation -> framework implication -> proposal` 四层分离；
2. 先记录 source-native objects/relations/conditions，再映射 VAF；
3. 冲突、反例、ambiguity 和 `NO EVIDENCE IN THE REVIEWED SOURCE POPULATION` 必须可见；
4. standard silence 不证明 novelty，也不授权任意设计；
5. 只进行 locator-backed 的最小仓库变更；
6. V0-V12 保持 `OPEN-CANDIDATE`，不得在 source task 中冻结 schema、metamodel、state machine 或 automation interface。

## Research contribution contract

This task answers RQ4/RQ5 by establishing the current assurance vocabulary and testing whether Parts 1 Clause 5–8 define assurance-case use, reasoning, evidence management, integrity/risk and lifecycle assurance strongly enough to constrain VAF claims. The study shall test for, rather than presume, a sufficiency reasoning algorithm.

## Candidate falsification tests

- `INN-T1`: `FALSIFY` only if the standard provides an equivalent general, executable sufficiency reasoning mechanism including residual uncertainty and authority context.
- `INN-T3`: test whether its evidence-management concepts already provide the same identity/provenance/applicability/credibility separation.

## Negative findings and non-answers

Preserve the 15026-2:2022 dated reference to 15026-1:2019 as opaque source-native provenance. Without the 2019 text, do not claim 2019/2025 semantic equivalence. Standard silence is `NO EVIDENCE`, not novelty.

## Detailed execution specification

### Execution outcome

Produce the controlled 2025 vocabulary foundation for the repository and a narrowly scoped compatibility decision for concepts actually imported from ISO/IEC/IEEE 15026-2:2022. This task is not a full 2019→2025 delta and must preserve Part 2's dated-reference provenance.

### Source and dependency gate

Confirm `references/PDF/15026-1-2025.pdf` title, edition/date, 36-page extent, SHA-256 and completeness. Inventory scope, normative references, terms, concept clauses, figures/tables and annexes with normative/informative classification. Snapshot every repository occurrence of `15026-1:2019`, `Claim type`, `claim`, `assurance`, `uncertainty`, `argument`, `evidence`, `confidence` and `assurance case` before editing.

### Mandatory research packages

1. **Scope and Clause 5 authority:** determine the role of Part 1 for Parts 2/3/4, multi-part use and authority, and what it does not prescribe.
2. **Clause 6 assurance concepts:** extract assurance case, reasoning method, evidence management, certification/accreditation, entities, relations, purposes, stakeholders and lifecycle context.
3. **Claim:** identify definition, subject/predicate structure if present, claim types/categories and relations to argument and evidence.
4. **Uncertainty and confidence:** distinguish source-native concepts, relations, treatment and any undefined framework additions.
5. **Assurance-case reasoning:** distinguish case, claim, argument, evidence, justification/context and assessment; determine whether a sufficiency algorithm exists rather than presuming one.
6. **Clause 7 integrity/risk:** extract integrity, risk and decision boundaries, including what remains authority-, context- or profile-defined.
7. **Clause 8 lifecycle assurance and family dependency:** record lifecycle relations and concepts explicitly shared by Parts 2, 3 and 4, with version-sensitive locators.
8. **Framework terminology audit:** identify collisions, narrower/broader usage and required `framework-defined` labels.

### Targeted 2019/2025 compatibility protocol

The compatibility review is limited to the concepts and relations used by the repository's Part 2 study, especially the source-native reference `15026-2:2022, 5.3.3 → 15026-1:2019, 3.1.4`. For each affected concept record: 2022 Part 2 locator and meaning; dated 2019 locator as cited; 2025 locator and meaning; relation (`compatible`, `compatible with qualification`, `changed`, `not found`, `not determinable`); repository impact; and wording required to preserve provenance.

Never rewrite the source-native locator. Where compatibility is not demonstrated, use: “Part 2 source-native provenance is the dated 2019 reference; the framework currently adopts the 2025 vocabulary, and equivalence is not presumed.”
