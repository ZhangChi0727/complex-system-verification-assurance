## Objective

Determine how assurance activities, claims, evidence and assurance cases are planned, maintained and integrated across the life cycle, including the software-life-cycle view.

## Required questions

- Which assurance activities and information relationships are normative, and how do they relate to 15026-1 concepts?
- How are assurance planning, maintenance, change, evidence and decision interfaces represented?
- What is the relationship to 12207:2026 and the software view used by 24748-3?
- Which findings affect V0, V10, V11 or V12 without defining a new universal workflow?

## Required work and outputs

Acquire/fingerprint the source; produce `../standard_notes/iso_iec_ieee_15026_4_2021_clause_study.md`; cross-map 15026-1/-2/-3, 12207 and VAF evidence/argument interfaces; update candidate gap scope and the Architecture Impact Register; submit for independent review.

## Stop conditions

Do not study or cite a DIS as the current standard. Do not freeze an assurance state machine or infer authority/acceptance semantics not present in reviewed clauses.

## Detailed execution specification

### Execution outcome and prerequisite

The work shall produce a clause-level assurance-life-cycle study grounded in the published 2021 edition. If only a draft, DIS, preview or catalogue abstract is available, update the acquisition blocker and stop. Task 003's reviewed 2025 vocabulary findings must be used as the current terminology layer; version tension must be recorded, not silently normalized.

### Source-control gate

Acquire the exact published edition, record canonical title, edition/date, page count, SHA-256, completeness and access provenance, and inventory all clauses/annexes with their normative status. Add the controlled source entry before extraction. Licensed source content must not enter Git.

### Mandatory research packages

Analyse separately:

1. scope, conformance, intended users and normative dependencies;
2. assurance objectives, assurance planning and integration with the life cycle;
3. claim, argument, evidence and assurance-case creation/use/maintenance;
4. assurance activities, responsibilities, independence or assessment roles where actually stated;
5. monitoring, change, impact analysis, re-assurance and evidence maintenance;
6. uncertainty, confidence, sufficiency and decision/acceptance boundaries;
7. software-life-cycle process interfaces and the relationship to 12207;
8. information items and their relationship to 15289 and Parts 1–3;
9. annex guidance/examples, explicitly prevented from becoming universal requirements.

### Extraction and classification

For every used proposition record exact locator/PDF page, source class, modality, paraphrase, objects/relations, preconditions, lifecycle timing, framework implication, confidence and destination. Classify the implication as `DIRECT SUPPORT`, `INDIRECT SUPPORT`, `PROFILE/CONTEXT`, `FRAMEWORK INTERPRETATION`, `RESEARCH PROPOSAL` or `NO IMPACT`.

### Required crosswalks

Produce:

- Part 1:2025 vocabulary → Part 4 native use → compatibility/qualification;
- Part 2 assurance-case structure → Part 4 lifecycle activity/information relation;
- Part 3 integrity-level concern → Part 4 assurance planning/rigor relation;
- Part 4 software view → 12207 process/interface dependency;
- Part 4 concept → V0/V10/V11/V12 and evidence architecture → limitation.

For every candidate lifecycle transition, identify initiating condition, activity, information consumed/produced, responsible role if specified, decision if specified and re-entry/change behavior. Absence of these elements prevents promotion to a universal state machine.

### Repository deliverables

Create `../standard_notes/iso_iec_ieee_15026_4_2021_clause_study.md`; update standards baseline, standards map, ISO-G03A/B, ISO-G05 and ISO-G07 as justified; update the Architecture Impact Register with reviewed dispositions; update HANDOFF and CHANGELOG; and create an independent-review packet containing source fingerprint, clause coverage, crosswalks, proposed changes, non-changes and open questions.

### Required final answers

State whether Part 4 changes the generic assurance lifecycle, only elaborates it, or provides a software-context profile; what it adds to evidence maintenance and change/re-assurance; whether any sufficiency or authority semantics can be promoted; and which dependencies must wait for 12207:2026 or other sources.

### No-overclaim rules

Do not treat guidance as a universal workflow, claim that an assurance case itself approves a system, equate process completion with sufficient assurance, or invent mandatory ownership/state/cardinality. Do not use a draft locator as the source for the published standard.

### Mandatory execution sequence and report structure

Execute in this order: verify prerequisites; acquire/fingerprint published source; inventory clauses; extract assurance activities and lifecycle relations; map Parts 1–3 and 12207; assess V0/V10/V11/V12; apply bounded deltas; run consistency checks; prepare review; stop.

The note shall contain: source/dependency control; clause inventory; terminology compatibility; assurance lifecycle and activities; planning; case/evidence maintenance; uncertainty/sufficiency/decision boundary; change and re-assurance; software-lifecycle interfaces; information items; crosswalks; V/gap/architecture effects; limitations; repository delta; conclusions and review handoff.

The review packet shall reconcile all primary clauses and lifecycle transitions, list proposed/non-proposed promotions, identify version tensions, enumerate changed files/statuses and include schema/state/authority overclaim checks.

### Definition of done

Done requires verified published source, complete clause inventory, all five crosswalks, locator-backed lifecycle conclusions, explicit version qualifications, synchronized repository state, a passed Markdown/link/diff sweep and independent review approval. A source-acquisition failure is a documented blocker, not a completed clause study.
