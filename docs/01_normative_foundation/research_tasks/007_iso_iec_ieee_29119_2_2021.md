## Objective

Extract the governance, management and dynamic-test process relations relevant to VAF planning, design, readiness, execution, evaluation, incident handling and completion.

## Required questions

- Which test processes, activities, inputs and outputs are normative?
- How do test planning, monitoring/control, design/implementation, environment, execution, incident reporting and completion interact?
- Which relations refine V0–V9/V12 only as a software-testing profile?
- What process iteration and re-entry semantics are present?

## Required work and outputs

Verify source identity; produce `../standard_notes/iso_iec_ieee_29119_2_2021_clause_study.md`; map process relations to V0–V12 with `Generic/Profile/No-impact` labels; coordinate Part 3 information items; record impact and independent review.

## Stop conditions

Do not rename or linearize V0–V12, generalize software test processes to all verification, or freeze workflow/state transitions.

## Research contribution contract

This task answers RQ2/RQ3/RQ5 by extracting full/tailored conformance and the organizational, test-management and dynamic-test process layers without converting diagrams into a mandatory sequence. Analysis/design shall use basis, test model, coverage items, techniques and traceability.

## Candidate falsification tests

Test `INN-A1/M1/M2` against iteration, monitoring/control, suspension/resumption, retest/re-entry and completion relations. An equivalent architecture or closure machine requires explicit authority/state semantics, not merely process completion.

## Negative findings and non-answers

Annex B alignment to 12207:2017 and Annex E test-model material are informative. Missing 12207:2017 makes edition-dependent alignment `NOT DETERMINED`. Completion does not equal V12 authority; process arrows do not impose universal time order.

## Generalization rights

The process model is a `Profile`; cross-domain iteration/change relations may be proposed for `Generic`; organization-specific roles and schedules remain `Practice`.

## Synthesis handoff dataset

Emit the common record plus `process_layer`, `conformance_mode`, `test_model_input_output`, `diagram_or_text_relation`, `iteration_reentry`, `completion_authority_limit` and `historical_alignment_status`.

## Detailed execution specification

### Execution outcome and dependency gate

Produce a clause-level software-test process profile and a bounded V0–V12 mapping. Begin only after Task 006 terminology is reviewed; if its vocabulary is still open, record the dependency blocker rather than inventing local definitions.

### Source gate and inventory

Confirm path, canonical edition/date, 64-page extent, SHA-256 and completeness. Inventory all clauses, process diagrams, tables and annexes with normative/informative status. For each process identify purpose, outcomes, activities, tasks, inputs, outputs and stated inter-process relations.

### Mandatory research packages

1. organizational, test-management and dynamic-test process architecture;
2. test planning, estimation, risk strategy, staffing and scheduling;
3. monitoring/control, status reporting, deviations and replanning;
4. test analysis/design: basis, test model, coverage items, techniques and traceability; historical `test condition` terminology is recorded only as an edition collision;
5. test implementation: cases, procedures, data, suites and readiness;
6. environment requirements, establishment, maintenance and configuration;
7. execution, result comparison, verdicts, logging and incident reporting;
8. completion, archiving, lessons and handoff;
9. iteration, repetition, suspension/resumption and change-triggered re-entry.

### Process extraction template

For every activity/task used, record locator/PDF page, modality, actor if specified, trigger/precondition, inputs, transformation, outputs, exit/completion criteria, traceability, upstream/downstream relations, tailoring, VAF profile mapping and confidence. Arrows in source diagrams must not be treated as mandatory temporal order unless the text says so.
