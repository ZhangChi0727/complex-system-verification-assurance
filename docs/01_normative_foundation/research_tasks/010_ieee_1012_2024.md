## Objective

Determine how IEEE 1012 structures V&V processes, integrity-dependent rigor, independence, task applicability and evidence across system, software and hardware life cycles.

## Required questions

- How are V&V integrity levels established and connected to task rigor?
- When and how is independence required, and what dimensions of independence are distinguished?
- Which tasks support coverage, anomaly handling, change/reuse and completion evidence?
- How does this rigor model compare with 15026-3 and aviation assurance levels without claiming equivalence?

## Required work and outputs

Verify source identity; produce `../standard_notes/ieee_1012_2024_clause_study.md`; create rigor/independence/applicability crosswalks to ISO and aviation profiles; reassess ISO-G01/ISO-G03B/ARP-G01 candidate scope; record impact and independent review.

## Stop conditions

Do not universalize every IEEE task, equate IEEE integrity levels with SIL/DAL/FDAL or claim certification acceptance.

## Detailed execution specification

### Execution outcome

Produce a clause- and table-level model of IEEE 1012 V&V applicability, integrity-dependent rigor and independence across system, software and hardware contexts. The report must make task applicability traceable rather than merely summarize the 309-page standard.

### Source gate and coverage control

Confirm path, canonical edition/date, 309-page extent, SHA-256, completeness and amendment status. Inventory every main clause, normative table, figure and annex. Because applicability may be encoded in tables, create a table inventory and verify every extracted row/footnote; do not rely only on prose or search hits.

### Mandatory research packages

1. scope, conformance, tailoring and relationship to life-cycle standards;
2. V&V concepts, processes, organization and plan;
3. integrity-level establishment, consequence/risk basis and level maintenance;
4. task applicability and rigor by integrity level, including table qualifiers and exceptions;
5. independence definitions, dimensions, required applicability and organizational/technical/managerial evidence;
6. system, software and hardware V&V task families across acquisition, development, operation, maintenance and retirement;
7. anomaly/problem reporting, corrective action, change impact, regression/re-verification and reuse;
8. V&V reporting, traceability, configuration, metrics, completion and approval boundaries;
9. tools/models and evidence credibility where explicitly addressed.

### Task/table extraction record

For each relevant task or table row record locator/PDF page, lifecycle context, integrity level(s), mandatory/conditional/recommended status, inputs, activity, outputs/evidence, responsible/independent role, completion criterion, tailoring rule, VAF mapping and limitation. Preserve footnotes and applicability conditions in paraphrase.

### Required mappings

- integrity level → task applicability/rigor, without cross-standard numerical equivalence;
- independence requirement → dimension → object/activity → evidence of independence;
- IEEE task → ISO 15288 process and V0–V12 concern;
- anomaly/change/reuse task → V9/V10/Evidence Provenance;
- completion/summary/approval → V11/V12 and Composite Gate separation;
- IEEE levels → 15026-3 levels → DAL/FDAL/IDAL, explicitly as comparison/non-equivalence.

### Repository deliverables

Create `../standard_notes/ieee_1012_2024_clause_study.md` with complete table traceability; update standards map and ISO-G01/ISO-G03B/ISO-G05/ARP-G01 only where supported; record Architecture Impact dispositions with migration notes; update baseline/HANDOFF/CHANGELOG; and create an independent-review packet whose checklist samples every applicability table and independence conclusion.

### Required final answers

State whether IEEE 1012 supplies a generic Assurance Applicability/Rigor mechanism, which independence semantics are defensibly generic, how task selection/tailoring works, and what remains domain/profile/authority-specific.

### No-overclaim rules

Do not equate integrity schemes, universalize highest-rigor tasks, infer certification credit, treat independence as one boolean, or claim task performance alone establishes sufficient assurance. Do not omit table footnotes or conditional applicability.

### Mandatory execution sequence and report structure

Execute in this order: verify source; build clause/table inventory; extract level model; extract all relevant task/applicability rows and footnotes; extract independence; map lifecycle/evidence/change/closure; compare other level schemes; update repository; run table reconciliation; prepare review; stop.

The note shall contain: source/conformance; clause/table inventory; V&V organization/process; integrity-level model; task applicability by context/level; independence dimensions/evidence; lifecycle task families; anomaly/change/reuse; reporting/configuration/completion; cross-standard rigor comparison; V/gap/architecture impacts; limitations; repository delta; conclusions and handoff.

The review packet shall reconcile every material table/footnote, show task population coverage, list all independence and non-equivalence propositions, changed files/statuses and reviewer samples across system/software/hardware contexts.
