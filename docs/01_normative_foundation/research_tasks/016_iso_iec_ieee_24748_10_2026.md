## Objective

Determine how systems-engineering agility affects iteration, re-entry, tailoring, feedback and dynamic-environment response in the open V0–V12 architecture.

## Required questions

- Which agility principles and lifecycle/application guidance are normative or informative?
- How are iteration, incremental delivery, feedback, learning, tailoring and change governed?
- Which findings affect V10 change impact, V6/V12 gates or process topology?
- What controls prevent agility from weakening evidence, traceability, independence or closure obligations?

## Required work and outputs

Reverify/fingerprint the acquired source; produce `../standard_notes/iso_iec_ieee_24748_10_2026_clause_study.md`; map Clause 4/5 findings to iteration/re-entry dimensions and ISO-G05/LC-G03; provide explicit compatibility/migration notes for any `MODIFY/SPLIT/MERGE`; obtain independent review before the freeze gate.

## Stop conditions

Do not use agility to waive assurance obligations, prescribe one method or freeze architecture before this task is independently reviewed.

## Detailed execution specification

### Execution outcome and freeze-gate role

Produce a clause-level agility/application study that stress-tests the open V0–V12 topology for iteration, feedback and dynamic change. This source must be independently reviewed before any final architecture freeze, but it cannot by itself waive normative obligations established elsewhere.

### Source gate

Verify the acquired published 2026 edition, canonical metadata, page count, SHA-256, completeness and annex status. Record that the document has no normative references and uses ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288/24748-1 as systems-engineering application context rather than making Task 005 a prerequisite. Inventory all Clause 4 terms, Clause 5 strategic aspects, tables, figures and examples; distinguish normative requirements, recommendations and explanatory agile practices.

### Mandatory research packages

Completely extract the Clause 4 source-native terminology and Clause 5 taxonomy. Each of the following eight strategic aspects is a separate package with purpose, source relations, selection/application guidance, evidence implications and limitations:

1. adaptable modular architecture;
2. iterative and incremental development;
3. attentive situational awareness;
4. attentive decision making;
5. common-mission teaming;
6. shared-knowledge management;
7. continual integration and test;
8. being agile.

Across the eight packages analyse uncertainty/dynamic-environment response, feedback/learning/adaptation, lifecycle/process tailoring, planning/replanning, stakeholder involvement, architecture/change control, verification/validation/evidence continuity, configuration/traceability, governance/decision cadence, risk/measurement, re-entry and completion. Annex A case story and Annex B Industrial DevOps are informative evidence only; neither may create mandatory workflow, roles, cadence, topology or tool selection.

### Iteration/re-entry extraction record

For each relevant proposition capture locator/PDF page, source class/modality, trigger, affected process/activity/information, feedback source, decision/authority if specified, state/baseline effect, evidence preservation requirement, re-entry target, VAF implication, migration risk and confidence.

### Required stress tests

1. Can V0–V12 represent multiple concurrent/iterative instances without renaming stable IDs?
2. What triggers V10 change-impact analysis and selective re-verification?
3. How do V6/V12 assessments and decisions operate for increments without becoming automatic releases?
4. Which evidence/provenance/configuration links must persist across iterations?
5. Which assurance obligations remain invariant under tailoring?
6. Does any source evidence require `MODIFY/SPLIT/MERGE`, or does the current open topology suffice?
