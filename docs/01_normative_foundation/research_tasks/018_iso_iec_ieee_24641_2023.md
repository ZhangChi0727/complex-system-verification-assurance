## Objective

Determine what the standard supports for model-based methods, tool use, model information, lifecycle integration and evidence, while separating method/tool guidance from qualification or admissibility claims.

## Required questions

- Which model-based methods, tool capabilities, information relations and lifecycle activities are addressed?
- What supports model traceability, consistency, completeness, analysis or verification evidence?
- Which claims remain outside scope: model credibility, tool qualification, executable semantics and certification acceptance?
- What candidate impact exists for ISO-G08 and VAF model-evidence interfaces?

## Required work and outputs

Reverify/fingerprint the acquired source; produce `../standard_notes/iso_iec_ieee_24641_2023_clause_study.md`; build the complete Plan/Build/Support/Perform matrix; cross-map ISO 15288 Annex D and VAF MBSE/DBSE concepts; keep 12207 rows provisional for Task 005; record candidate evidence/provenance relations and independent review.

## Stop conditions

Do not treat model existence as evidence sufficiency, infer tool qualification or commit to a platform technology.

## Research contribution contract

This task answers RQ3/RQ5/RQ7/RQ8 through the exact source groups `Plan MBSSE`, `Build models`, `Support models` and `Perform MBSSE`, plus Clause 4 conformance and Clauses 5–9. It shall separately evaluate model correctness, fitness/credibility, tool trust/qualification, result reproducibility and evidence admissibility.

## Candidate falsification tests

Test `INN-T3/M5/I1` against explicit model/tool/result control relations. Organization `shall implement` and method/tool `should support` have different force; only equivalent general admissibility conditions can falsify INN-M5.

## Negative findings and non-answers

Model V&V in 7.4/7.5 is distinct from system V&V in 9.8. Model or simulation results are not automatically admissible evidence. 12207:2017 mapping remains `NOT DETERMINED` until the source is controlled.
