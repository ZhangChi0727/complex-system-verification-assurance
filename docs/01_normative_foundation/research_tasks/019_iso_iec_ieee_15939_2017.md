## Objective

Study measurement information needs, measures, collection, analysis and communication as candidate support for evidence characterization and coverage/sufficiency assessment inputs.

## Required questions

- How are information needs, measurement constructs, data collection, analysis and decision support related?
- Which measurement records require provenance, configuration and quality controls?
- Which concepts can support evidence metrics without defining universal sufficiency thresholds?
- How do measurement results differ from verification results and Evidence Items?

## Required work and outputs

Recheck revision status and reverify/fingerprint the acquired source; produce `../standard_notes/iso_iec_ieee_15939_2017_clause_study.md`; complete the bounded 15288 mapping; map measurement constructs to Evidence Item characterization and V11 inputs; update candidate gaps without closing sufficiency; record impact and independent review.

## Stop conditions

Do not turn measures into claims automatically, prescribe universal thresholds or equate quantitative coverage with assurance sufficiency.

## Research contribution contract

This task answers RQ3/RQ4/RQ5 through normative Clause 4 conformance, Clause 5 application and Clause 6 Measurement Process, with Annexes A–G all registered as informative. It shall extract `information need → measure → indicator → decision criterion` and classify uncertainty, accuracy, repeatability, reproducibility and fitness/confidence as information-product quality.

## Candidate falsification tests

Test `INN-T1/T3/M3` against explicit measurement quality, provenance and coverage semantics. A measure, threshold or indicator falsifies a sufficiency/coverage candidate only if equivalent argument and decision semantics are present.

## Negative findings and non-answers

Information-product quality does not equal argument sufficiency. Missing 15288:2015 makes old-to-current mapping rows `NOT DETERMINED`; 2023 locators cannot replace source-native provenance. All Annex A–G are informative.
