## Objective

Study measurement information needs, measures, collection, analysis and communication as candidate support for evidence characterization and coverage/sufficiency assessment inputs.

## Required questions

- How are information needs, measurement constructs, data collection, analysis and decision support related?
- Which measurement records require provenance, configuration and quality controls?
- Which concepts can support evidence metrics without defining universal sufficiency thresholds?
- How do measurement results differ from verification results and Evidence Items?

## Required work and outputs

Acquire/fingerprint the source; produce `../standard_notes/iso_iec_ieee_15939_2017_clause_study.md`; map measurement constructs to Evidence Item characterization and V11 inputs; update candidate gaps without closing sufficiency; record impact and independent review.

## Stop conditions

Do not turn measures into claims automatically, prescribe universal thresholds or equate quantitative coverage with assurance sufficiency.

## Detailed execution specification

### Execution outcome

Produce a clause-level measurement-process and measurement-information model that can characterize evidence and inform V11 assessments without inventing universal assurance thresholds.

### Source gate and inventory

Acquire and verify the published 2017 edition, metadata, page count, SHA-256, completeness and annex status. Inventory every process/activity/task, information model, measure definition, table, figure and annex. Separate normative requirements from measurement examples.

### Mandatory research packages

Analyse organizational/project measurement context; information needs and decision objectives; measurement concept/model; base measure, derived measure, indicator and decision criterion; data collection/storage; analysis and interpretation; communication/reporting; measurement planning and commitment; evaluation/improvement of the measurement process; data quality, validity, uncertainty, provenance and configuration; stakeholder/decision roles; and tailoring.

### Measurement extraction record

For each construct or task capture locator/PDF page, source class/modality, information need, entity/attribute, measure/scale/unit, method/function/model, data source, collection condition, analysis/indicator, criterion, uncertainty/quality control, output/decision use, VAF mapping and limitation.

### Required mappings

1. information need → measure → indicator → decision input;
2. measurement data/result → Verification Result → Evidence Item distinction;
3. measure provenance/quality → Evidence Provenance characterization;
4. coverage population/criterion/measure → ISO-G02A/B;
5. measurement indicator → Sufficiency Assessment input → authority/threshold limitation;
6. change in information need/baseline → V10 measurement impact.

### Repository deliverables

Create `../standard_notes/iso_iec_ieee_15939_2017_clause_study.md`; create a measurement-to-evidence crosswalk and candidate characterization fields; update standards map, ISO-G02B/ISO-G03B and Architecture Impact Register only as supported; update baseline/HANDOFF/CHANGELOG; prepare independent review.

### Required final answers

State which measurement constructs are generic, which evidence metrics are only framework proposals, how data quality/provenance constrain use, and why a measure or threshold does not itself prove a claim or sufficiency.

### No-overclaim rules

Do not auto-promote measurement outputs to Evidence Items, universalize thresholds/units, equate coverage with confidence, or treat an indicator as an authority decision. Do not ignore scale, collection context or uncertainty.

### Mandatory execution sequence and report structure

Execute in this order: acquire/verify source; inventory constructs/processes; extract the measurement information model; extract activities/tasks; map evidence and V11 uses; test threshold/sufficiency boundaries; update repository; run measure/claim checks; prepare review; stop.

The note shall contain: source/control; clause/construct inventory; information needs; measurement model; base/derived measures and indicators; collection/data quality/provenance; analysis/criteria/communication; process evaluation; six VAF mappings; evidence-metric candidates; gap/architecture effects; limitations; repository delta; conclusions and handoff.

The review packet shall reconcile all source-defined constructs and primary tasks, list each candidate field and unsupported threshold, changed files/statuses, and verify that no measure was automatically promoted to claim/evidence/sufficiency.

### Definition of done

Done requires verified source, full clause/construct inventory, six mappings, locator-backed evidence-metric boundaries, synchronized repository changes, clean link/diff/table checks and independent review approval.
