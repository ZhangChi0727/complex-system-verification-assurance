# SAE ARP4754B Reading Notes

## Public metadata confirmed

- Title: *Guidelines for Development of Civil Aircraft and Systems*.
- Revised 2023-12-20; DOI 10.4271/ARP4754B.
- Public scope includes requirements validation and verification of design implementation for safety, certification and product assurance.
- Public scope points detailed software, electronic hardware, IMA and safety assessment processes to DO-178C, DO-254, DO-297 and ARP4761A respectively.

## Clause-level review queue

- aircraft/system requirements and derived requirements;
- requirements validation versus design implementation verification;
- development assurance planning, FDAL/IDAL and independence;
- integration and verification data;
- unintended behavior and lifecycle evidence;
- interfaces to ARP4761A, DO-178C, DO-254 and DO-297.

## Extraction rule

Record aircraft/system-level objectives without importing item-level objectives unless the standard explicitly establishes the relationship.
